# Read this before you publish a single video

Making an animated **kids** channel is the hardest niche to monetize per view. None of this
blocks the idea — it just shapes how you should run it.

## 1. "Made for Kids" is the law, not a setting
Under COPPA, content "directed to children" MUST be marked **Made for Kids**. This pipeline sets
`madeForKids: true` on every upload. Consequences you must accept:
- **No personalized ads** → RPM drops hard (often **$0.05–$0.30** vs $1–$5+ elsewhere).
- **No comments, no end screens, no cards, no community posts, no notifications to subscribers.**
- Your income model is therefore **volume + watch-through**, not RPM. Short, high-retention,
  binge-able loops — produced in batches — are the whole game.

Do **not** mislabel kids content as not-made-for-kids to get better ads. YouTube/FTC penalties
are severe and channel-ending.

## 2. Monetization threshold (verify current numbers when you start)
YouTube Partner Program, Shorts path:
- **1,000 subscribers + 10,000,000 valid Shorts views in 90 days**, or the long-form path
  (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours).
- Earlier fan-funding tier: ~500 subs + 3M Shorts views / 3,000 watch hours.

## 3. AI / "inauthentic content" policy (2025–2026)
Faceless AI content is allowed, but YouTube demonetizes low-effort, mass-produced spam. You must:
- **Disclose AI-generated / synthetic content** (there's a checkbox at upload; also honest in
  the description).
- Keep genuine quality: good writing, a consistent voice, a consistent visual style, real value
  for the viewer. This is exactly why the pipeline keeps **you** in the loop on two approvals.

## 4. Practical guardrails baked into this pipeline
- The story prompt forbids anything scary/violent/sad-ending/commercial.
- Approval #1 (script) and Approval #2 (final video) are your quality gates — don't remove them.
- Consistent character descriptions across scenes keep the animation coherent (looks less "AI slop").

## 5. If the economics disappoint
Same pipeline, different audience = very different income. "Family / all-ages folklore, fables,
mythology, bedtime stories for tweens+" is **not** Made-for-Kids, so personalized ads are allowed
and RPM is far higher. If pure-kids RPM frustrates you, that's the pivot — no code changes needed
beyond flipping `madeForKids` to `false` **and genuinely making the content for a broader
audience** (you must be truthful about who it's for).
