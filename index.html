02 — About

The analyst behind the dashboards.

I've spent six years learning that the hardest part of analytics isn't the query — it's making the answer impossible to ignore.

I'm a data and analytics leader based in Toronto, currently Manager of Data Analytics & Reporting at the Condominium Authority of Ontario, where I lead the team and platform that turn regulatory, compliance and sector data into public-facing transparency and better decisions for Ontario's condo communities.

Before the public sector, I spent four years at Walmart Canada as Lead Analyst for BI Development & Analytics — architecting data pipelines, ETLs and dashboards across Power BI, Looker and Tableau that leadership relied on daily. Earlier, at Amazon, I re-engineered marketplace operations with data-driven automation.

Across every role the pattern is the same: I sit between raw data and the people who have to act on it, and I make that gap disappear — through clean modelling, governed pipelines, sharp visualization and a relentless focus on the metric that matters.
